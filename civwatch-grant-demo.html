Why It Exists

The Information Void

American citizens — mothers, fathers, people who belonged here — were taken, and the public watched through a news feed that told half the story depending on which algorithm fed it to them. That's not an accident. It's the design.

⚠️
ICE Block Was Removed
The tool that gave vulnerable communities real-time awareness of enforcement activity was quietly deplatformed. The people who need protection most are always the first stripped of the tools to protect themselves.
💰
Money Flows Are Hidden
PAC networks, lobbying pathways, and donor chains are technically public but practically invisible. No single tool connected the dots across contributions, legislation, and outcomes — until now.
🕸️
Division Is Manufactured
Politicians share donors, dinners, and interests while publicly performing opposition. The network behind the theater is never shown. CIVWATCH maps it.
📡
No Neutral Ground Existed
Every civic platform carried a partisan load — by design or algorithm. There was no place where a red voter and a blue voter could look at the same verified data. That place now exists.

Why It Can't Be Killed

No Kill Switch

Designed from the ground up to survive deplatforming, subpoenas, and the removal of any single developer, server, or store.

🔗
IPFS Storage
No single server to subpoena. Civic records, cluster outputs, and historical data are distributed across the IPFS network. Content-addressed storage means data survives node failures.
🌐
Browser-Native
TensorFlow.js runs scoring entirely in the browser. No app store approval. No Play Store. No App Store kill switch. If you have a URL, you have access — period.
🔓
Open Source
No single developer whose disappearance ends the project. The codebase, the schema, the ML models — all public, forkable, and community-owned from day one.
