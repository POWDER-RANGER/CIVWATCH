Who is watching my town, who paid for it, and how do I challenge it?

CIVWATCH is an open-source civic intelligence project. Its working application, CIVINTELLIGENCE, follows surveillance technology from the street corner to the contract that paid for it and the public record you can request about it. Every claim links to its source.

How claims are handled

The people who need this most, reporters, council members and defense attorneys, need to cite it. That sets the rules.

Every line has a source

Each record keeps the public URL it came from and when it was retrieved. When a source can't be reached, the page says so instead of filling the gap.

A mapped camera is a report

Camera locations come from community mapping. They show where a device was reported, not proof that it is operating today.

Status words mean one thing

Live means public and usable. Available means built and connected. In progress means being built. Planned means not started.

Build on it

All code is MIT licensed. The REST API behind the web application is public, and the native clients for Android, iOS, Windows and Linux are being built on the same contract rather than a separate backend.
