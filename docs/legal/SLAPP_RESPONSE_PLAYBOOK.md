# CIVWATCH SLAPP Response Playbook

> **Document Classification**: LEGAL-DEFENSE | **Review Cycle**: Quarterly | **Effective Date**: 2026-06-24
> **Preparedness Target**: Response protocol tested within 48 hours of any legal threat

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Strategic Lawsuit Against Public Participation (SLAPP) suits are a primary legal threat to CIVWATCH. This playbook ensures rapid, coordinated response to any legal action designed to silence, intimidate, or burden the platform. Every hour of delay increases risk and cost.

> **Key Principle**: CIVWATCH does not negotiate with SLAPP filers. We respond with maximum legal force, public transparency, and community solidarity.

---

## 2. SLAPP THREAT CLASSIFICATION

### 2.1 Threat Levels

| Level | Indicator | Response Time | Escalation |
|-------|----------|---------------|------------|
| **WATCH** | Cease-and-desist letter; verbal threat; social media threat | 24 hours | Legal counsel review; document preservation |
| **ALERT** | Draft complaint shared; pre-litigation demand | 12 hours | Activate SLAPP response team; notify partners |
| **CRITICAL** | Complaint filed and served; TRO requested | **2 hours** | Full response protocol; emergency motions; public statement |
| **EMERGENCY** | Asset freeze; injunction granted; law enforcement action | **Immediate** | All-hands; emergency counsel; EFF emergency hotline |

### 2.2 Common SLAPP Patterns

| Pattern | Filer Type | Typical Claims | Response Strategy |
|---------|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| **"Defamation" SLAPP** | Politician, lobbyist | Published analysis is "false and damaging" | Anti-SLAPP motion; truth defense; public record citations |
| **"Privacy" SLAPP** | Donor, corporate officer | Aggregation "invades privacy" | Public record defense; aggregation thresholds; First Amendment |
| **"Copyright" SLAPP** | Government agency | Policy documents are "protected" | Public record; government edicts doctrine; fair use |
| **"Tortious Interference" SLAPP** | Lobbying firm | Transparency "harms business" | First Amendment petition right; anti-SLAPP; Noerr-Pennington doctrine |
| **"Harassment" SLAPP** | Police union | Monitoring "harasses officers" | Public interest defense; qualified immunity does not apply to records |
| **"Data Protection" SLAPP** | Foreign agent (FARA) | Publication violates GDPR/privacy laws | U.S. jurisdiction; public record; SPEECH Act defense |

### 4.2 Key Arguments (California)

| Argument | Legal Basis | Evidence |
|----------|------------|----------|
| **Publication is protected speech** | CCP § 425.16(e)(3) — "any written or oral statement or writing made in a place open to the public or a public forum in connection with an issue of public interest" | CIVWATCH publishes on public website; all data from public records |
| **Data collection is protected petition activity** | CCP § 425.16(e)(2) — "any written or oral statement or writing made before... a judicial proceeding" | FOIA requests are formal petitions to government |
| **Plaintiff cannot show probability of prevailing** | CCP § 425.16(b)(1) | Truth defense; public records; no actual malice |
| **Mandatory attorney fees** | CCP § 425.16(c)(1) | Document all fees for recovery |
| **Discovery stay** | CCP § 425.16(g) | Immediate discovery stay upon filing motion |

### 4.3 Federal SPEECH Act Defense (28 U.S.C. § 4101-4102)

If a foreign judgment is obtained against CIVWATCH:

| Element | Application |
|---------|------------|
| Foreign defamation judgment | CIVWATCH can seek declaratory judgment that foreign judgment is not enforceable in U.S. |
| Foreign law inconsistent with First Amendment | U.S. court shall not recognize/enforce |
| Personal jurisdiction | CIVWATCH can file in U.S. district court |

## 5. EMERGENCY INJUNCTION DEFENSE

### 5.1 Temporary Restraining Order (TRO) Response

| Step | Timeline | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| 1 | 0-2 hours | Receive TRO; immediately notify Legal Lead and Incident Commander |
| 2 | 2-4 hours | Legal Lead reviews TRO; assess scope; identify overbreadth |
| 3 | 4-8 hours | Draft emergency opposition; file for hearing |
| 4 | 8-24 hours | File opposition; request expedited hearing; notify partners |
| 5 | 24-48 hours | Hearing; argue against TRO; present First Amendment defense |
| 6 | 48-72 hours | If TRO granted: appeal; maintain operations within scope; public statement |

### 5.2 Key Arguments Against Prior Restraint

> *"Any system of prior restraints of expression comes to this Court bearing a heavy presumption against its constitutional validity."* — **Organization for a Better Austin v. Keefe**, 402 U.S. 415 (1971)

| Argument | Legal Basis |
|----------|------------|
| **Prior restraint presumptively unconstitutional** | *Near v. Minnesota*, 283 U.S. 697 (1931); *New York Times v. United States*, 403 U.S. 713 (1971) |
| **Public records cannot be enjoined from publication** | *Oklahoma Hospitality Club v. State*, 742 P.2d 120 (Okla. 1987) |
| **No imminent irreparable harm** | Plaintiff's harm is reputational, not imminent safety threat |
| **Less restrictive alternatives exist** | Correction, clarification, right of response — not prior restraint |
| **CIVWATCH is not state actor** | Cannot be bound by government speech restrictions |
